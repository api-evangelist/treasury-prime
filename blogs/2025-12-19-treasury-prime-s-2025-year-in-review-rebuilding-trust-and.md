2024 was a turning point as BaaS programs unraveled, new rules under the GENIUS Act took shape, and the Synapse fallout made direct oversight of every account and transaction essential. In 2025, Treasury Prime used its BankOS platform and bank network to help banks and fintechs move from firefighting to safe, auditable embedded programs. This recap explains what we built, how we showed up in the industry, and what it means for 2026 planning.
